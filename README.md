# 🏰 **Shades Mansion**

**Shades Mansion** is a game that blends **board strategy** with a **dungeon-like atmosphere**.  
Three sides — **light**, **gray** and **dark** — compete inside mysterious mansions with redrawn floor plans every match. One side will conquer the current mansion.  

---

## 🎮 **Gameplay Overview**
- Turn-based tactics on a grid, inside a **procedurally generated maze**  
- **Three teams** play with a distinctive dynamic  
- Different **characters**, different moves  
- **Items** to be collected and used strategically  

---

## ♟️ **Characters**

Every team fields similar character types, each type with distinct mechanics.

---

## 🕯️ **Items**

The mansion holds valuable items for each of the character types,  
so much that they can be tricked and manipulated over them.  
It's all a matter of strategy.  

---

## 🎲 **Commanding side(s)**

Before the match you choose what to command:  

- **One team** — the other two are played by the AI  
- **All of them** — local multiplayer  
- **None** — spectate a match between three AI teams  

---

## 📖 **Library**

A library menu holds the lore, a source of subtle hints and immersion for the game.  

---

## ⚙️ **Options**

Some settings can be tweaked to experiment with the game.  

---

## 🧰 **Tech Stack**
- **React + TypeScript**  
- **React Router** for screen navigation  
- **Vite** for fast development  
- **Material UI (MUI)** for interface components  
- **Phaser** for the characters, items and movement animations drawn over the board
